Details

Stays on your device

Your video is never uploaded. Everything happens in this tab, so private footage stays private.

Just a short clip as a guide

Phones forget how the video was recorded when they die. A few seconds of any good video from the same phone tells Reel Rescue how to put yours back together.

Built for big files

Reads the file in small pieces, so hour-long, multi-gigabyte recordings don't freeze your browser.

What happened to your video

While recording, your phone saves the picture first and the "table of contents" last. If the battery dies in between, the picture is all still there, but the player has no way to find it. Reel Rescue finds it again and writes a new table of contents.
